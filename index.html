How enrolment works

1 Keep your NSEP details ready

Your NSEP Registration Number and Date of Birth, exactly as registered for NSEP.

2 Submit the enrolment form

At vdnt.in/NSEP26VOS. Details that don't match your NSEP registration can't be processed.

Enrolment is processed within 72 hours of submitting the form. Watch your registered email, including spam.

            Tests & the exam

            What the camp prepares you for

            NSEP is the National Standard Examination in Physics, conducted by the Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT). It is Stage I of India's Physics Olympiad programme.

            NSEP 2026 · per IAPT

            Sunday, 22 November 2026 8:30–10:30 AM · at your NSE centre

            Section A

            48 MCQs

            One correct option · +3 / −1

            Section B

            12 MCQs

            One or more correct · 6 marks, only if all correct options and no wrong option are marked

            Total216 marks · 2 hours
            1. I
              NSEP · 22 Nov 2026

              Stage I, conducted by IAPT. The camp's tests are built on this paper's pattern. IAPT expects Stage I results by 25 December 2026.

            2. II
              INPhO · 31 Jan 2027

              Stage II, the Indian National Physics Olympiad, as announced by HBCSE. Camp students who qualify move to the VOS INPhO Batch.
